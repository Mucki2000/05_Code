About

A small crew
with a high bar.

Lausebengel Cuts is a video editing and social media studio from Switzerland. The name (Lausebengel means "rascal" in German) is the brief. We bring a kid's mischievous joy to the work and a sharp sense for quality. You only get one with the other from us.

For the right joke, the story matters more than the format. Whether it's a brand film, a Reel or a multi-part campaign. Every project starts with the same question: what stays with the audience once the clip ends?
